About Us

Your trusted partner in electrical excellence

Smart Solution Team

Smart Solution Electrical Trading & Projects (Pty) Ltd

Founded by Tumelo Penny Rabopape, Smart Solution has grown to become one of South Africa's most reliable electrical service providers. We specialize in delivering top-tier electrical solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

Our mission is to provide safe, efficient, and innovative electrical services that power progress while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional electrical services that exceed client expectations through innovation, safety, and reliability.

Our Vision

To be the leading electrical solutions provider in South Africa, known for quality, integrity, and excellence.

Our Values

Safety, Integrity, Excellence, Innovation, and Customer-Centric approach in everything we do.

Our Services

Comprehensive electrical solutions for every need

House Wiring

Residential electrical installations, rewiring, and upgrades. Complete home electrical solutions from new builds to renovations.

  • New installation wiring
  • Rewiring old houses
  • Distribution board upgrades
  • Lighting installations

Fault Finding

Expert diagnosis and troubleshooting of electrical issues. We identify and fix problems quickly and safely.

  • Circuit breaker tripping
  • Power outages diagnosis
  • Short circuit detection
  • Earth leakage problems

Motor Connections

Single and three-phase motor wiring, installation, and maintenance for industrial and commercial applications.

  • Single-phase motor wiring
  • Three-phase motor installation
  • Star-delta starters
  • Motor control panels

Panel Design & Wiring

Custom electrical panel engineering, design, and installation for various applications.

  • Distribution panels
  • Control panels
  • MCC panels
  • Custom panel builds

PLC Wiring

Programmable Logic Controller installations, programming, and integration for automation systems.

  • PLC panel wiring
  • Automation systems
  • Sensor integration
  • Industrial controls

Maintenance

Routine service and repairs for all electrical systems to ensure optimal performance and safety.

  • Preventive maintenance
  • System inspections
  • Emergency repairs
  • Compliance certificates

Electronics

Installation and service of geysers, stoves, and other household electrical appliances.

  • Geyser installation & repair
  • Stove & oven connections
  • Appliance wiring
  • Air conditioning circuits

Solar & Inverter Systems

Solar power and battery inverter installations for energy independence and cost savings.

  • Solar panel installation
  • Inverter systems
  • Battery backup systems
  • Grid-tied solutions
